aiDataWorks is an AI-first data management partner, Informatica Platinum Partner, and Salesforce partner. We deliver full lifecycle outcomes — assess, modernize, govern, and activate — as a managed service, not a staffing pool.

What we do

Modernize the foundation, govern it, and build the AI that runs on it

Enterprises are under pressure to deliver AI-driven outcomes, yet most still run on fragmented, on-premise data estates never built for the cloud or for machine learning. Data sits siloed across systems, governance is inconsistent, and every initiative inherits the same technical debt. We close that gap — modernizing legacy platforms for the cloud, embedding governance and quality by design, and making data AI-ready so leaders move from reactive to confident decisions.

Why now

AI is a board mandate, and the cloud window is closing

Measurable AI outcomes are expected, ungoverned data is the No. 1 blocker, and legacy platforms are nearing end-of-life — modernization can't keep slipping. Standing still means run-rate costs on infrastructure that caps growth, not reduces it.

  • Fragmented estates — critical data locked in legacy, on-premise systems with no single trusted view
  • Stalled AI initiatives — analytics and AI programs fail to scale because data isn't governed, clean, or accessible
  • Rising operating cost — manual integration and aging infrastructure inflate run-costs
  • Governance & compliance risk — inconsistent quality, lineage, and controls expose the business to regulatory risk
